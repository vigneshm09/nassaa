Who We Are

More Than Just a Gaming Zone: Chennai's Premier Indoor Entertainment Hub

Nassaa Uth Hub is the world's first youth entertainment center, and Chennai is where it all comes alive. Designed for every age group, every mood, and every squad size, Nassaa Uth Hub Chennai brings together gaming, sports, and immersive experiences that you simply won't find anywhere else in the city.

Choose from VR gaming, arcade machines, bowling, snooker, PlayStation stations, futsal courts, escape rooms, laser tag, and more, with over 15 activities across our 3 Chennai branches at Besant Nagar, OMR, and Injambakkam. Whether you're planning a family outing, a birthday bash, or a weekend hangout with friends, there's always something dialed up to full throttle at Nassaa.
