Brewklyn Café

Brewklyn is the beating heart of NASSAA UTH HUB — a fully equipped café designed for gamers, families, and anyone who needs to refuel between sessions. The menu spans fresh-brewed specialty coffees, chilled mocktails, creamy milkshakes, and a rotating selection of quick bites: loaded fries, wraps, mini-burgers, waffles, and more. The seating area doubles as a lounge where you can watch your friends compete on screens mounted around the space. Whether you are waiting for your bowling lane, cooling off after laser tag, or just here for the chill atmosphere, Brewklyn delivers the vibe.

Good to Know

  • 01
    Open to All Visitors — You don't need to be booked for an activity to use Brewklyn. Walk in, grab a table, and order. Open during all NASSAA UTH HUB operating hours.
  • 02
    Order at the Counter — Place your order at the main counter. You'll receive a token number and your order will be brought to your table when ready.
  • 03
    Gaming Combos Available — Ask about bundle deals that include a café item with an activity booking — often better value than ordering separately.
  • 04
    No Outside Food or Drinks — Outside food and beverages are not permitted inside the café area. This ensures quality and hygiene for all guests.
  • 05
    Celebrate Here — Brewklyn is available for birthday setups, group celebrations, and small events. Contact us in advance to arrange decorations and a reserved area.
  • 06
    Tables Are Shared During Peak Hours — During weekends and holidays, seating may be shared. Keep the space clean and make room for others when you are done.
