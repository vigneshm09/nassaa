Who We Are

Built for Every Kind of Fun

NASSAA UTH HUB was born from a single vision: to create a space where entertainment knows no boundaries — no age limits, no experience required, just pure, authentic fun.

We are proud to be the world's first youth entertainment center, offering a carefully curated mix of high-tech gaming, classic sports, and immersive experiences all under one roof. What started as a bold idea in Chennai has grown into three thriving branches — each one designed to be a home away from home for the youth of Tamil Nadu.

At Nassaa, we don't just offer games. We offer memories. From first-timers learning snooker with our patient coaches, to hardcore gamers grinding on our premium PC rigs — every visitor leaves with a story worth sharing.

Our founder, Mr. Nassar, personally coaches visitors on snooker and 8-ball pool — a tradition that has become one of the most beloved aspects of the Nassaa experience. That personal touch, that warmth, is woven into everything we do.
