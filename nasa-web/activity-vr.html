How It Works

  • 01
    Pick Your Experience — Choose from rotating VR titles: solo adventures, co-op missions, or competitive battles. New experiences added regularly.
  • 02
    Gear Up — Staff fits your VR headset and motion controllers. Takes 2 minutes — no tech knowledge needed.
  • 03
    Stay in the Play Zone — A marked boundary defines your safe space. Your headset shows a guardian grid near edges — respect it.
  • 04
    No Running or Jumping — Your real-world vision is blocked by the headset. Walk, don't sprint. Protect yourself and the equipment.
  • 05
    Handle Equipment with Care — Don't yank cables, drop controllers, or remove the headset mid-session. Report discomfort immediately.
  • 06
    Age & Health Note — Recommended for ages 7+. Not suitable for epilepsy, severe motion sickness, or recent eye surgery.
  • 07
    Session Time — Standard sessions are 10–15 minutes per game. Take a 5-minute break between back-to-back sessions.

Pro Tips

⚡

Go Multiplayer

Shared VR experiences are objectively more chaotic and way more fun. Book a duo slot and watch your friend freak out.

🎯

Try Something Scary

Your brain can't tell a virtual cliff from a real one. VR horror is the hardest flex in gaming. Do it once.

💡

Beat Motion Sickness

Pick stationary experiences first (VR escape rooms, archery). Fast-flying games hit harder — build up gradually.
