About the Marina Mall Branch

Which floor is Nassaa Uth Hub in Marina Mall?

Nassaa Uth Hub is located on the entertainment floor of OMR Marina Mall, Egattur. The mall directory at the entrance has directions.

Is parking available at OMR Marina Mall?

Yes, OMR Marina Mall offers free basement parking for visitors. Two-wheelers and cars are both accommodated on-site.

How far is it from Sholinganallur?

OMR Marina Mall at Egattur is approximately 4 km from Sholinganallur junction — about a 10-minute drive via Old Mahabalipuram Road.

Book at Nassaa Uth Hub Marina Mall

Perfect for post-work team outings and weekend family plans — book your gaming session at Nassaa Uth Hub Marina Mall or simply walk in. On-site staff assist with all 4 activities, no prior experience needed.
