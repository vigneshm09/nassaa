Who We Are

Built for Every Kind of Fun at Chennai's Premier Youth Entertainment Center

Before Nassaa opened its doors in Chennai, there was no such thing as a youth entertainment center. Mr. Nassar changed that by building a space from the ground up where entertainment has no age limit, no experience requirement, and no ceiling on fun.

Nassaa is proud to hold that title as the world's first youth entertainment center, built right here in Chennai. We brought together high-tech gaming, classic sports, and immersive experiences under one roof, and what started as a bold idea in Besant Nagar in 2017 has grown into three thriving branches across Chennai, each one designed to be a home away from home for the youth of Tamil Nadu.

At Nassaa, we don't sell tickets. We build memories. From the first-timer nervously picking up a snooker cue to the seasoned gamer logging hours on our premium PC rigs, every visit produces a story worth sharing. With 15+ unique experiences spread across our branches, there is always something new to try, and always a reason to come back.

Our founder, Mr. Nassar, still steps onto the floor to personally coach visitors on snooker and 8-ball pool. It is a tradition that has become one of the most talked-about parts of the Nassaa experience, proof that behind every great gaming zone is a team that genuinely cares about the people walking through the door.
